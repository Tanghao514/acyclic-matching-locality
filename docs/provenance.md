# Provenance and scope

## Frozen implementation

The four modules under `code/` come from the project's frozen block-first v8
implementation, used by the 16 September 2026 B/C experiments. They are copied
without changing any bytes. Their SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`.
The original environment record also lists modules from a broader internal
experiment package. Modules unrelated to this manuscript are not included.

## Original measurements

The five JSON files under `data/original/` are unchanged archived inputs,
answers, timings, and environment information. There are 26 controlled
parameter configurations and 14 direct-enumeration comparison cases. In the
manuscript these support Tables 1--7; the small-instance answers were rechecked
in the revision. Three repeated timings are not independent graph samples.

## Revision checks

The two files under `data/revision/` are unchanged archived outputs from the
19 September 2026 revision. They include 14 original rechecks, 48 boundary and
cost cases, all 76 labelled simple base graphs of orders zero through four,
and four comparisons against root/parent enumeration (Table 8). The independent
oracle is in `validation/run_checks.py`; it tests actual induced subgraphs
and legal matching extensions. Its tiny-graph helpers assume consecutive
integer labels, as generated by the suite. It is not a general-purpose oracle API.

## Public-runner adaptations

- Algorithm imports now use `code/`; archived inputs use `data/original/`.
- New outputs go to `runs/revision/` or `runs/parameters/`, never archived data.
- Revision result dates describe the new run, not a hard-coded historical date.
- The revision runner asserts that both original input lists contain 14 cases.
- Historical timing runners fail with an explanatory message on platforms
  without `SIGALRM`; use Linux/WSL for these runners.

No mathematical algorithm, case generator, cost seed, or archived measurement
was changed. Original result files contain hashes of original scripts;
adapted scripts necessarily have different hashes. The release manifest records
both source and public hashes. Fresh timings should not be substituted for
historical manuscript values without updating the manuscript's environment.

## Interpretation

Checks cover exact values, optimal and total counts, optimal witnesses, root-set
characterizations, ordinary/acyclic unweighted optimum equality, cardinality
spectra, and subdivision obstruction lengths on the listed finite instances.
They neither establish general mathematical claims nor constitute independent
third-party verification. Parameter timings illustrate tested families, not
uniform superiority over all alternative algorithms.
